Pak-Plaza is a catalog, not an authorship claim. Every pak here was made by the NextUI and
MinUI community. Pak-Plaza only gathers them by device and, for some, provides a build adapted
to the RG353V. Credit belongs to the people and projects below.

  https://github.com/LoveRetro/NextUI and https://nextui.loveretro.games
* **MinUI**, the launcher NextUI is built on, originally by **Shaun Inman**

  (https://github.com/ro8inmorgan/MinUI).
* **The NextUI Pak Store**, by **LoveRetro**, which this catalog is modelled after.
